Section 07

Key Insights

What the numbers actually mean
01
LinkedIn drove the highest hiring volume. 20 of 76 total hires (26%) originated from LinkedIn — the largest single channel, though its 4.9% conversion trails smaller channels.
02
Employee Referrals convert best once an offer is made. Referred candidates accept offers at 80%, well above LinkedIn (52%) and Naukri (50%).
03
Sales has the longest hiring cycle — 50.8 days on average vs. 40.1 for Operations, the fastest department.
04
Freshers are the largest hiring segment — 30% of all joiners, ahead of every other experience band.
05
Sarah Thomas leads on volume; Mohammed Ashraf leads on quality — 17 hires vs. a team-best 68.4% acceptance rate.
Section 08

Strategic Recommendations

Reduce the Sales hiring cycle

Auditing redundant interview rounds could bring Sales closer to the 40-day Operations benchmark.

Impact: faster time-to-productivity

Expand the employee referral program

Referrals convert at 80% offer-acceptance vs. ~52% for LinkedIn — scale incentives accordingly.

Impact: lower cost-per-hire

Rebalance sourcing spend away from Consultancies

Only 1.2% conversion from 83 applications — the weakest channel in the funnel.

Impact: improved sourcing ROI
